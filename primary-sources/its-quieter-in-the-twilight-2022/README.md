## Why it's here

The documentary is the most thorough public record of the late-stage
Voyager team — the second-wave engineers who joined for Uranus
encounter (1986) and stayed through the Interstellar Mission. Their
on-camera reflections are primary-source oral history about:

- The institutional knowledge problem ("most of us are single point
  failures")
- The Voyager 2 power-subsystem row/column command corruption (a
  separate failure mode from the Voyager 1 FDS memory anomaly)
- The DSS-43 downtime (March 2020 – February 2021) when Voyager 2
  was uncommandable
- The geographic and demographic diversity of the team (engineers
  from Mexico, South Korea, Colombia, Tuskegee, etc.)
- The 2030 power-margin endpoint

See [`/analysis/voyager-team-history.md`](../../analysis/voyager-team-history.md)
for the research-relevant content paraphrased and cited.

## Use status

The .srt file was retrieved from OpenSubtitles, where it has been
publicly accessible since the documentary's home-video release.
Subtitle files for commercial films exist in a long-tolerated
gray zone of fair-use accessibility / language-learning / research
distribution.

We're including it here:
- As the **captions/transcription only** — the documentary itself
  is **NOT** redistributed; users must rent / stream / buy it from
  the legitimate distributors above
- For research-archive and accessibility purposes, consistent with
  fair-use norms in academic and library contexts
- With proper attribution to the filmmaker and full pointers to
  the official distribution channels

If the filmmakers (Billy Miossi or representatives) prefer this be
removed, contact via [itsquieterfilm.com](https://itsquieterfilm.com/)
or open an issue on this repository.
