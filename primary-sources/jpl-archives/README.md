# JPL Archives — Voyager Project finding aids

## What's in this folder

- `JPL634_Voyager_Project_Collection.pdf` — the complete finding
  aid for **JPL Collection 634, Voyager Project Collection,
  1966–1990.** 184 pages. Lists every folder in 258 letter-sized
  Hollinger boxes plus 6 narrow boxes plus 9 oversize boxes
  (≈104.68 cubic feet of paper documents and engineering drawings)
  organised into 19 topical series.

## How to use this

Find the folder you want by grepping the finding aid for keywords,
note the Series number and Folder number, then email
**archives@jpl.nasa.gov** to request access.

The collection is held at the JPL Archives in Pasadena, CA. Access
is by appointment for outside researchers; many records can be
delivered electronically by request.

## Access restrictions

The finding aid notes the collection is "Restricted Partly" — JPL
DISCREET files within the collection (a small subset, six folders
mentioned in Series 16) are accessible only to JPL employees and
contractors. The vast majority of the collection is requestable by
outside researchers.

## Use status

Finding aids themselves are public records that JPL publishes for
research access. We redistribute it here verbatim as a navigation
aid, not as a substitute for going through proper request channels
when materials are needed.
